Compiled from general knowledge, not checked against live sources. Ownership and headquarters change, and some entries are judgement calls. Treat it as a starting list to verify.

## What the list shows

- **Strong:** L2 to L3, the machines that make chips. This is where Europe has near-monopoly positions (ASML, Zeiss, Trumpf).
- **Thin:** L4 has strong chip makers, but no leading-edge foundry or HBM producer in the list.
- **Thin:** L7 has one frontier-class lab (Mistral). The others are either owned by non-European parents or work in narrower areas.
- **Borderline:** L6 is the weakest-sourced layer. Data and talent do not map neatly onto companies.
- **Ownership caveats:** Arm is UK-headquartered but majority-owned by SoftBank. Google DeepMind is part of Alphabet.
