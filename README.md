# EU AI

A small project to understand where Europe sits in the AI industry stack.

## Inspiration

This started from Stubb's talk: [YouTube, from 13:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TU9JRW27vrQ&t=794s).

## Approach

1. **Visualize the stack.** Map the AI industry as a dependency tree, like a civilization tech tree. It runs from raw materials (rare elements, metals) through chip machines, chips, data centres and frontier labs, up to applications and end users. See [`ai_stack_tech_tree.md`](ai_stack_tech_tree.md), which is a Mermaid diagram.
2. **Look at the EU companies that contribute to it.** With the stack laid out, place European companies on the layers they serve, and see where Europe is strong and where it depends on others. The diagram already highlights a few starting points, such as lithography, optics, lasers, chip IP and frontier labs. A fuller company-by-layer list is the next step.
